Distraway — media playback

Google Play review demonstration for FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK.

The user taps Play, leaves the app, and controls the ongoing playback from its notification. Next sound switches the sound. Stop ends playback and removes the foreground notification.

This is a screen-only recording of the signed release APK (1.6, version code 7) on Android 16. The video does not contain an audio track. Playback and screen-off continuation were separately checked against Android audio playback state.

Audio is synthesized locally, starts only after a user action and remains audible while using other apps or turning the screen off. Delaying or interrupting the foreground task would unexpectedly delay or stop the focus sound the user requested. Playback can be stopped from the app or the notification.

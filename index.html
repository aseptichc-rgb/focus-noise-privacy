이 정보는 회원 생성, 로그인 상태 유지 및 계정 식별을 위해 사용됩니다. 인증 서비스는 네트워크 요청을 처리하기 위해 IP 주소 등의 연결 정보를 받을 수 있습니다. 소리 재생 기록과 뽀모도로 설정은 계정 서버로 전송하지 않습니다.

이용자가 고른 뽀모도로 집중 시간, 몰입 모드 설정과 첫 사용 안내 완료 여부를 기기 내부에 저장합니다. 몰입 모드를 해제할 때 복원하기 위해 필요한 기존 방해 금지 설정도 일시적으로 저장합니다. 이 설정은 기기 밖으로 전송하지 않습니다. 로그인한 경우에는 Firebase SDK가 인증 상태를 기기에 저장합니다. 로그아웃하면 앱의 로그인 세션이 해제되지만 서버의 회원 계정이 삭제되지는 않습니다.

Ritono Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Ritono (formerly Distraway and 집중 백색소음, the “app”) requires Google or Apple sign-in. Sound playback works offline while you remain signed in. Sign-in processes account information as described below.

1. Personal data collection

Sign-up and sign-in use Firebase Authentication and your chosen provider (Google or Apple) to process your account identifier, email address, and name and profile photo URL when provided. The app displays your name and email but does not display your photo. Apple Hide My Email may supply a relay address. The app does not directly receive or store your Google or Apple password or collect location, contacts or call history.

Account information is used to create accounts, maintain sign-in sessions and identify members. Authentication services may receive connection information such as an IP address when processing network requests. Sound playback history and Pomodoro settings are not sent to an account server.

2. Information stored on your device

Your selected Pomodoro duration, Focus mode setting and first-use tutorial completion are saved locally. Previous Do Not Disturb settings needed for restoration are temporarily saved locally. These settings are not transmitted. When signed in, the Firebase SDK also stores authentication state on your device. Signing out ends your app session without deleting your server account.

3. Permissions

  • Internet: enable account sign-up and sign-in. Sound playback works offline while you remain signed in.
  • Notifications: show playback status, timer countdown and playback controls.
  • Foreground service (media playback): keep playing sounds when your screen is off.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): allow calls while temporarily suppressing other notification sounds, vibrations, pop-ups and alarms in Focus mode. Stopping playback or turning Focus mode off removes the restriction applied by the app. The app does not read or collect notification contents, contacts or call history.

4. Third parties and advertising

The app has no ads or analytics. Account authentication uses Google’s Firebase Authentication and your chosen Google or Apple authentication service to process necessary account information. See Firebase privacy information, Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for the providers’ data handling.

5. Account retention and deletion

Firebase account records are retained until the account is deleted. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete server records. Request deletion from Account → Request account deletion in the app, or use the account deletion instructions on this page. Firebase account information is deleted after account ownership is verified. Your original Google or Apple account is not deleted; those providers handle their records according to their own policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. Please contact the email below if you learn that a child’s account information has been registered.
